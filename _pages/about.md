I am a postdoctoral researcher in the <a href='https://climca.site'>climate attribution group</a> of Sebastian Sippel at the Institute for Meteorology, Leipzig University. I work on statistical methods for probabilistic forecasting and extreme events, with applications in weather and climate science. Current topics include the verification of AI weather models for extremes, and rare event algorithms that use AI models to guide sampling.

I completed a PhD in Statistics at the University of Exeter in 2026, in partnership with the UK Met Office and The Alan Turing Institute, working on statistical post-processing and probabilistic forecasting methodology. Before that I studied data science at UCL, mathematics and physics at TU Berlin, and philosophy and political science at FU Berlin. I am passionate about methods and tools for open and usable weather and climate information. Together with Fiona Spuler I developed the [ibicus](/software/) package for the bias adjustment of climate models.
