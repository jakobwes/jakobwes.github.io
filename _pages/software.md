## ibicus

A Python package for the bias adjustment of climate models and associated evaluation. It provides a common interface to a range of methods, including ISIMIP3BASD, quantile mapping and CDF-t, together with diagnostics to evaluate trend preservation and marginal, multivariate and spatiotemporal properties. ibicus is used in academia and industry. Development was funded through the ECMWF Code for Earth programme.

[GitHub](https://github.com/ecmwf-projects/ibicus) · [Documentation](https://ibicus.readthedocs.io) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/ibicus/) · Paper: [Spuler, Wessel et al. (2024), GMD](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-17-1249-2024)



## wxsbi

A Python toolkit for stochastic weather generators, built on NumPyro, and for adjusting them to target summary statistics using methods from simulation-based inference. This is useful for stress-testing in scenario-neutral impact modelling approaches. Development was funded through the Helmholtz Centre for Environmental Research – UFZ and the Helmholtz Information and Data Science Academy (HIDA).

[GitHub](https://github.com/bgroenks96/wxsbi) · Paper: [Groenke, Wessel et al. (2026), JGR-MLC](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JH000902)
