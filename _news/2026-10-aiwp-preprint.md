New preprint: we compare [probabilistic AI weather models with the ECMWF IFS ensemble on heat extremes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20519), including record-breaking heat extremes. When evaluated probabilistically, the AI models outperform the NWP ensemble on these events.
